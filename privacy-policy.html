OptiSec Mobile · by OPTISEC
Last updated: [DATE]

OptiSec Mobile ("the App") is developed by OPTISEC. This policy explains what data the App accesses, why, and what happens to it.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your Password Vault entries. All vault data is encrypted locally on your device (AES-256-GCM) and never leaves it.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not require an account to use core security features.

Permissions We Request and Why

PermissionPurpose
Location (Wi-Fi scan)Required by Android to read nearby Wi-Fi network details (BSSID/SSID) for Evil Twin / rogue access point detection. Coordinates are not stored or transmitted.
Usage AccessBuilds the Permission Usage Timeline locally, showing which apps access sensitive permissions and when. Stays on-device.
BiometricUnlocks the Password Vault and App Lock via Android Keystore/BiometricPrompt. The App never receives or stores raw biometric data.
NetworkPowers the Threat Intelligence Feed and any cloud feature you explicitly enable.
StorageStores encrypted vault data and app settings locally.

Data Stored Locally On Your Device

  • Encrypted password vault entries
  • Wi-Fi network trust-score history
  • App lock PIN (hashed, never stored in plaintext)
  • Permission usage history (for the timeline feature)

This data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup to prevent it from being restored onto another device without your explicit action.

Threat Intelligence Feed

The App downloads publicly available threat intelligence data from our servers/GitHub feed. This is a one-way download — no personal or device-identifying data is sent beyond standard network request metadata.

Optional Cloud Features

If you opt in to threat-sharing or cloud sync, only the minimum data required (e.g. anonymized threat indicators) is transmitted. Disabled by default.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features change. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
